# Porter's Five Forces (Competitive Industry Analysis)

## What it is
Porter's Five Forces analyzes the structural drivers of industry profitability and competitive intensity across five dimensions: (1) Rivalry among existing competitors, (2) Threat of new entrants, (3) Bargaining power of buyers, (4) Bargaining power of suppliers, (5) Threat of substitute products or services. In consulting, it is used to establish why the client's competitive environment makes the proposed engagement urgent and to benchmark the client's position against structural industry dynamics.

## When to use it
- The engagement involves competitive strategy, market positioning, or pricing
- The client faces new competitive threats (new market entrants, digital disruptors)
- The problem involves supplier or customer relationship management
- You need to establish external urgency: "the industry structure is shifting against you"

## Consulting language
"We will conduct a structural analysis of [Industry] competitive dynamics, assessing the key forces shaping [Company]'s competitive position…", "The competitive analysis will identify the structural forces creating pressure on [Company]'s [margin / market share / pricing power], forming the external context for our recommendations…"

## Customization guidance
- Standard Five Forces is almost always too generic. Always rename forces to reflect the specific industry
- For technology/AI-heavy industries: add a sixth dimension — "Algorithmic Intensity of Competition" (ML capabilities, data moats, AI deployment speed of competitors)
- For regulated industries: split "Threat of substitutes" into "Direct substitutes" and "Regulatory disruption"
- For B2B services: weight "Buyer power" most heavily (it typically dominates)
- Compress forces where context makes them irrelevant: a monopoly engagement might reduce to 3 relevant forces
- Use as input to the SWOT, not as a standalone deliverable — connect each force to a specific hypothesis

## Example deliverables in a proposal
- "A customized competitive forces analysis for [Industry], identifying the 3 structural dynamics most relevant to [Company]'s strategic challenge"
- "An assessment of [Company]'s competitive positioning across [relevant forces], benchmarked against key competitors"
- "A competitive threat prioritization matrix linking structural forces to specific strategic vulnerabilities"
