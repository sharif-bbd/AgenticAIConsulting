# PESTLE Analysis (Macro-Environmental Scanning)

## What it is
PESTLE examines six macro-environmental forces that shape an organization's strategic context: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, and Environmental. In consulting proposals, PESTLE is used to establish the external "burning platform" — demonstrating that forces beyond the client's control are creating a window of opportunity or threat that makes action urgent now. It answers: "Why can't the client wait another 12 months?"

## When to use it
- The engagement involves strategy in a rapidly changing regulatory or technological environment
- The client is skeptical about the urgency of the problem — PESTLE provides objective external evidence
- Industry disruption is a key driver (new regulations, AI wave, sustainability mandates)
- The problem statement requires establishing macro context before diving into company-specific issues

## Consulting language
"We will develop a PESTLE analysis identifying the macro-environmental forces most relevant to [Company]'s strategic challenge…", "The external context analysis will assess [specific PESTLE dimensions] and their implications for [Company]'s [competitive position / cost structure / go-to-market]…", "A structured review of regulatory developments, technological shifts, and market trends will establish the external imperative for the proposed program."

## Customization guidance
- Do NOT use all six dimensions equally — this signals generic thinking. Select 2–4 that are genuinely material
- Rename dimensions to be client-specific: "Technological" → "AI and Automation Disruption" for a manufacturing client
- Always connect each relevant PESTLE factor directly to the client's stated problem and timeline
- Use PESTLE to create urgency: "Regulatory change X takes effect in [date], reducing the window for action to [N] months"
- Source external quadrants from real data: regulatory announcements, analyst reports, competitive moves
- Pair with a "So what?" column: each PESTLE factor should link to a strategic implication

## Example deliverables in a proposal
- "A macro-environmental analysis identifying the [N] external forces most material to [Company]'s strategic challenge and timeline"
- "An external context assessment covering [specific selected dimensions], with implications for [Company]'s decision timeline"
- "A regulatory and technology landscape review establishing the external imperative for [program name]"
