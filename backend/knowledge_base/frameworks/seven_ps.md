# The 7Ps Framework (Service Marketing Mix)

## What it is
The 7Ps extend the classic 4Ps of marketing (Product, Price, Place, Promotion) with three additional dimensions critical to service businesses: People, Process, and Physical Evidence. In consulting, the 7Ps are used for go-to-market strategy, service design, customer experience transformation, and operational improvement engagements. They ensure that a strategic recommendation addresses both the market-facing elements (what you sell and how) and the operational elements (how you deliver it).

## When to use it
- The engagement involves go-to-market strategy, sales effectiveness, or market entry
- The client is redesigning a service offering or customer experience
- The problem involves a disconnect between strategic intent and operational delivery
- The client needs to understand why customers are not purchasing/renewing/recommending

## Consulting language
"Our methodology will assess [Company]'s service model across its core dimensions — from offer design through delivery and evidence of value…", "We will evaluate [Company]'s go-to-market approach, identifying misalignments between strategic positioning and operational reality…", "The analysis will cover the full service value chain, from [offer] through [channel] to [customer experience], identifying the highest-value improvement levers."

## Customization guidance
- Always compress to the relevant Ps: for a B2B SaaS company, "Physical Evidence" becomes "Product experience and trust signals"
- Rename each P to the client's language: "Place" → "Channel strategy" or "Distribution model"
- Prioritize based on the probing meeting: if the client flagged "our sales process is broken," weight Process and People most heavily
- Use the 7Ps to structure the deliverables: each P becomes a diagnostic question and a potential improvement lever
- In efficiency engagements: 7Ps can be reframed as "7 structural levers for service cost and quality"

## Example deliverables in a proposal
- "A diagnostic assessment of [Company]'s service model across [N selected dimensions], identifying the highest-value improvement opportunities"
- "A go-to-market effectiveness analysis covering offer design, channel strategy, and sales process"
- "A service delivery review assessing the alignment between [Company]'s strategic positioning and its operational capabilities"
